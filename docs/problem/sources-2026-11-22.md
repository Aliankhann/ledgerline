## CRA (canada.ca)
- T5008 Box 20 page (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/tax-slips/understand-your-tax-slips/t5-slips/t5008-statement-securities-transactions-slip-information-individuals.html, modified 2025-12-11):
  "The amount in box 20 may or may not reflect your adjusted cost base (ACB)" … "You are required to make the adjustments, as needed".
- Superficial loss (…/line-12700-capital-gains/capital-losses-deductions.html, "Superficial loss"; same in T4037 ch. 5):
  window "starting 30 calendar days before the sale and ending 30 calendar days after the sale"; still owns "30 calendar days after the sale";
  affiliated: spouse/common-law partner, corporation controlled by you or spouse, partnership & majority-interest partner, trust & majority-interest beneficiary.
  "If you are the person who acquires the substituted property, you can usually add the amount of the superficial loss to the adjusted cost base of the substituted property."
  No worked numeric example. No statement on TFSA/RRSP repurchase (secondary sources only: TaxTips, advisor.ca, RBC WM Nov 18 2026 — loss denied permanently).
- Identical properties (…/line-12700-capital-gains/special-rules-other-transactions.html; T4037 ch. 3): "Dispositions of identical properties do not affect the ACB."
  Example STU Ltd.: 2001 buy 100 for $1,500 (ACB/sh 15.00); 2006 buy 150 for $3,000 → $4,500/250 = 18.00; 2008 sell 200 for $3,600 (ACB/sh stays 18.00);
  2025 buy 350 for $7,350 → ($900 + $7,350)/400 = $8,250/400 = 20.63. Commissions and the 2008 gain not stated.
- Gain example (…/calculating-reporting-your-capital-gains-losses.html): 2025-03-13 sold 400 XYZ for $6,500, commission $60, ACB $4,000 → gain $2,440, taxable $1,220.
- Mutual fund page (…/completing-schedule-3/tax-treatment-mutual-funds.html): redeem 200 units at $17.42 = $3,484, fee $70, ACB 200 × $15.20 = $3,040 → gain $374.
  ROC: units bought $1,000 (2013); $200 ROC each year 2016–2020 → ACB 0; further $100 ROC this year → $100 capital gain (line 13200), ACB stays 0.
- ACB includes acquisition expenses "such as commissions and legal fees"; selling outlays are subtracted together with ACB from proceeds.
- FX: proceeds at "the exchange rate in effect at the time of the sale", ACB at time acquired, outlays when incurred; same page also allows annual average
  "if there were transactions at various times throughout the year" (inconsistent). Folio S5-F4-C1 ¶1.3–1.4: Bank of Canada rate for the day the amount arises
  (or closest preceding day); ¶1.6.1 averages only for certain income items. ITA s. 261(2)(b): "relevant spot rate for the day on which the particular amount arose".
- Trade vs settlement date: NOT stated by CRA ("calendar year … that you sell"). Practitioner (RBC DS blog) uses settlement date for the 30-day window.

## CRA technical interpretation 2004-0073011E5 (2004-09-22; read on taxinterpretations.com, third-party host)
- "the Canada Revenue Agency administratively accepts that the amount of the 'superficial loss' may be determined" by SL = (Least of S, P and B)/S × L
  (S sold, P acquired in the 61-day period, B left at end of period, L loss). Facts: held 50, bought 10 in June 2003 (60), sold 50 on 2003-07-01 at a loss,
  bought 10 within 30 days after, holds 20 at end → S=50, P=20, B=20 → loss on 20/50 denied, added to ACB of the 20 shares under 53(1)(f). No prices given.
  Caveat in TI: may not represent CRA's current position.
- Secondary numeric variant (advisor.ca "How superficial loss rules impact employee plans"): S=50, P=20, B=60, L=$750 → SL = $300, $450 claimable.
